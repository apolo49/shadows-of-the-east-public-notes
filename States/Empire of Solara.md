The **Empire of Solara**, also known as the **Solari Empire**, is the dominant imperial power of the eastern reaches of [[Shul]] in the current era, and the political backdrop against which the events of _[[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/index|Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East]]_ take place. Its capital is [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Amphos|Amphos]], and its territory stretches across numerous provinces, client cities, and border marches, administered through a network of fortified keeps and garrisoned by a standing military under the command of the [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/NPCs/Amphos/The Court of Amphos/Zenshoran Hagashar|Donpotes da Tagaminase]].

## Overview

Solara presents itself as a vast, ancient, and unified imperial power, though the party's travels have suggested an empire held together as much by administration, garrisons, and diplomacy as by any deep cultural unity. Its cities range from the grandeur of the capital, Amphos, to smaller coastal ports like [[Pithertara]], each maintaining its own keep, garrison, and local economy while answering to the imperial centre. At the time of the party's travels, the Empire is in a state of heightened diplomatic tension, following the disappearance of [[Ellowen Aurelian Vale|Lady Ellowen Aurelian Vale]] of [[Duchy of Brackenfall|Brackenfall]] — an event now treated as a formal diplomatic incident, given her betrothal to [[The Prince - Player Notes|the Prince]].

## History

### The Division and the refounding of Amphos

According to an inscription on a weathered obelisk standing in the market square of Pithertara, Amphos was refounded as the imperial capital by [[Anparashi Puli Bralofai Blafadasi Komitani Ailushi|Puli Komitani]] approximately seven hundred years before the present day. It was at this time that the administration of the empire was formally relocated to Amphos. The obelisk itself bears further writing, apparently favoured personally by Puli Komitani, that is illegible to virtually everyone in the modern era — "forgotten by all but the learned," in the words of one Pithertaran local.

Similar standing obelisks are attested elsewhere in and beyond Solari territory, including one associated with the "[[Obelisk of Origin]]" in the distant land of [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Solaum|Solaum]], and reportedly another in the holy city of [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Itrea|Itrea]]. Their true origin and purpose remain unclear, though they appear connected in some fashion to the elemental stones now carried by members of the party.

### The present day (1782 IOG)

As of the party's travels through Pithertara, the Empire is in the midst of a significant military redeployment, with soldiers being reassigned in unusually rapid succession — from Amphos, to Pithertara, to [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Mormegalia|Mormegalia]], and now toward Itrea — a shift attributed by rank-and-file soldiers to unclear orders "from the top." This redeployment coincides with a diplomatic mission being undertaken by the Prince to Itrea, ostensibly for reasons of protection, though the exact purpose has not been made clear to the common soldiery escorting him.

Simultaneously, the disappearance of Lady Ellowen Aurelian Vale — taken, so far as imperial record-keepers are aware, from Amphos itself while under Solari protection — has become a matter of state importance, [[Alaric Aurelian Vale - Player Notes|her father]] having formally reported her missing to the crown. As she was recently betrothed to the Prince, her disappearance carries the weight of a genuine diplomatic crisis between Solara and the Duchy of Brackenfall.

## Government

### The Emperor and the Prince

Solara is ruled as a hereditary empire. The current Emperor is not named in any record so far encountered by the party, and is referred to informally by at least one Solari officer simply as "the king" — whether this reflects a genuine second title, common soldierly imprecision, or simple habit is unclear. His heir, referred to throughout Solari territory only as **the Prince**, is described by those who know him as "quite nice, but incredibly intrusive," with a tendency to involve himself in problems — or create them — and rely on [[Tabtab|a close, and reportedly tabaxi, companion]] to resolve them. The Prince is currently betrothed to Lady Ellowen Aurelian Vale of House Aurelian Vale, of the Duchy of Brackenfall, and is presently travelling to Itrea on what has been described as a diplomatic mission.

### Administration

Each major Solari settlement is organised around a keep — a fortified administrative complex housing not only military garrisons but the civil apparatus of the empire: record-keepers, ledgers, and officials empowered to issue travel documentation. Keeps of any size typically contain, within their walls, the full infrastructure of a small settlement in miniature — smithies, butchers, storage, and armouries — surrounding a central administrative hall of considerable age and ornamentation.

Travel between Solara and neighbouring or client territories requires official papers, colloquially called "pass-ports", issued at a keep's administrative hall. Standard passage — for example, from Pithertara to the Duchy of Brackenfall — has been priced as high as fifty gold, reflecting both the distance involved and the current diplomatic climate; discounts are available to veterans of Solari military service, verifiable via service badges issued at border postings such as [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Akrossa|Akrossa]]. Once issued, such papers carry the Empire's formal guarantee of safe passage, with any harm coming to the bearer treated as grounds for serious diplomatic repercussions against the offending territory.

## Military

The Solari military is headed by an officer bearing the title Donpotes da Tagaminase, presently held by Zenshoran Hagashar, a tabaxi who is said to have come from [[Divine Empire of Daxing|another country]] entirely before rising to his current position after befriending the crown some twenty years prior. He is one of only two tabaxi confirmed by the most recent Amphos census — a count that notably excludes the slum districts of the capital, where at least one more tabaxi is known to have lived, unrecorded.

Soldiers of the Solari military are routinely posted across the breadth of the Empire and beyond — Amphos, Pithertara, Mormegalia, and, increasingly, Itrea — often with little notice or explanation given to the rank and file. Veteran status, marked by badges such as the one issued at the Northern Border with the [[Duchy of Dornsia]], carries recognised privileges even outside active service, including reduced fees for civil administrative matters such as travel documentation.

### Seas and pilgrimage routes

The [[Moli Aeianao]] lies to the south of Pithertara and forms the primary maritime pilgrimage route toward the holy [[City State of Itrea|City State of Itrea]] and the entrance to the [[Mount Aemi]] trial — a mountain pilgrimage attempted by many but completed successfully by few. The [[Moli Aurirorinao]], further west, lies along the alternate river-and-road route toward Solaum.

## Foreign relations

### Duchy of Brackenfall

Solara maintains close, if currently strained, diplomatic ties with the [[Duchy of Brackenfall]], cemented by the betrothal of the Prince to Lady Ellowen Aurelian Vale, whose family — House Aurelian Vale — maintains a mercenary keep in Amphos considered a valuable asset to the capital. Ellowen's recent disappearance has placed considerable strain on this relationship.

### Duchy of Dornsia

Solara shares a guarded northern border with the [[Duchy of Dornsia]], marked by the military crossing at Akrossa, where veterans of border service have received badges recognised throughout the Empire, such as the one given to [[John Ironchest]].

### Caliphate of Lqlima

Far to the south lies the [[Caliphate of Lqlima]], a land regarded with suspicion within the Empire — whether fairly or as a matter of state propaganda remains, at least to some travelling Solari citizens, an open question. [[Ulriki]], a young traveller taken in by John Ironchest, hails from Lqlima and journeyed north seeking a life he felt he could not have there.

## Religion, society, and culture

### The office of Puli

The title Puli recurs throughout Solari and pre-Solari history, attached to figures of apparent religious or civic authority — among them Puli Komitani, credited with refounding Amphos, and the [[First Puli]], a figure of considerable antiquity who is said to have "trusted" the elemental stones now carried by the party, and whose voice has been heard directly by at least one of them. The exact nature and modern status of the Puli office is not fully understood, though its historical importance to the founding of the current imperial order appears significant.

### The World Bodybuilding Order

The **[[World Bodybuilding Order]] (WBO)** operates at least one chapter within Solari territory, having hosted a strongman exhibition in Pithertara's market square featuring competitors in atlas stone lifting, wrestling, squats, keg tossing, and deadlifting. Its structure and reach beyond individual host cities remains unclear.

## Calendar and currency
*Main Article: [[Setting/Semantics/Dating/index|Dating]]*

The Solari calendar divides the year into thirteen months, including Atmi and Hanomi; years are dated using the suffix **IOG**, with the present year recorded as 1782 IOG. Currency in circulation includes gold, electrum, and silver coinage, with prices for goods, services, and official documentation varying considerably by province and circumstance.
