**Tolaunena Pashor** is an incendiary substance developed and employed by the [[Empire of Solara|Eastern Solari Empire]] for use as naval ordnance, most notably as fuel for the ship-mounted siphon weapons carried aboard Solari warships such as [[The Zoraneset|the Zoraneset]].

The recipe for Tolaunena Pashor is a closely-guarded state secret.

## Overview

Tolaunena Pashor is the Solari military's standing incendiary weapon, carried aboard warships for use against enemy vessels via bow-mounted siphon emplacements. Little is recorded of its preparation process, which appears to be closely held knowledge within Solari naval circles, but its volatility are well enough known to the sailors who live alongside its stores.

## Composition

The substance is described as a rudimentary mixture of petroleum, honey, tree sap, and resins. The honey, sap, and resin content is generally understood by those who have handled it to serve as a thickening and adhesive base, allowing the mixture to cling to whatever surface it strikes rather than simply running off, while the petroleum component supplies its fuel. The result is a substance that burns readily, persistently, and with considerable danger to anything — or anyone — nearby.

## Military use

Tolaunena Pashor is loaded into a ship's siphon and expelled under pressure, projecting the burning mixture at range against enemy vessels, fortifications, or personnel. As a weapon, it is prized for the same qualities that make it hazardous to store: its adhesion, its intensity, and the difficulty of extinguishing it once alight.

### Aboard the Zoraneset

Stocks of Tolaunena Pashor are carried aboard the Zoraneset for use in her bow-mounted siphon. Crew familiar with her cargo have noted, not without unease, that the substance's flammability poses a standing risk to the ship herself — a single serious accident involving her stores could, in principle, turn the vessel into a pyre for her entire complement of some two hundred and fifty souls. No such incident has occurred to date.
