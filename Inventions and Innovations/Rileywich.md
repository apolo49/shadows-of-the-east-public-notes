The **Rileywich** is a sandwich, and small but growing family of sandwich variants, invented by [[Riley - Player Notes|Riley]], cook of the [[The Zoraneset|Zoraneset]] and the [[Elemental]] of [[Elements#Fire Stone|Fire]]. It remains, at present, the primary documented culinary credential behind his appointment to that role.

# Variants

## Original
Created by placing cuts of cured meat between slices of bread at the [[The Heavy Head Inn]] on the road to [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Aklebe|Aklebe]], Riley created the original Rileywich here to much fanfare of the party, excluding [[Big Beefy Bob - Player Notes|Big Beefy Bob]] who preferred his gruel.

## Grilled Rileywich
The best-documented variant to date, the **Grilled Cheese Rileywich**, was invented under less than ideal circumstances at an inn in [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Pithertara|Pithertara]]. Riley had woken to find his face frozen to a bowl of cheese and bread left out overnight — the unintended result of a stray Ray of Frost cast by a sleeping [[Ellowen Aurelian Vale|Ellowen]] — and, on her suggestion, used his own fire stone to thaw himself free. The heat melted the cheese directly onto the table in the process, and Riley, mid-crisis, claimed the result on the spot as a new variation on his existing recipe. His thanks to Ellowen for the assist were noted at the time as heavily, and audibly, sarcastic.

## Garlic Toast Rileywich
During the crossing from Pithertara toward [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Myrneleu|Myrneleu]], Riley was recorded producing **toasted garlic rileywiches** for the crew, stretching limited stores of bread and grain into something more palatable than the alternative gruel on offer. Whether this dish constitutes a further Rileywich variant, or a separate recipe entirely, has not been formally confirmed — Riley has not been asked directly, and no one has yet thought to check.

# Reception
No formal reviews of the Rileywich, in any of its known forms, have been recorded. Given the general standard of shipboard cooking aboard the Zoraneset — most notably the crew's recurring gruel — expectations among the crew are understood to be, at minimum, easily cleared.
