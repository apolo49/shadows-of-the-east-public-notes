**Pithertara** is a coastal city of the [[Empire of Solara]], and the administrative seat of [[Pithertara Province]]. With a population of just over eight thousand, it is the largest settlement the party had visited besides the imperial capital itself except [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Itrea|Itrea]], and served as their point of departure for the leg of [[The Zoraneset|the Zoraneset's]] voyage south toward [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Myrneleu|Myrneleu]] and, ultimately, [[Itria Province]].

## Overview

Pithertara sits on the coast along the party's route between [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Aklebe|Aklebe]] and Itria Province, and is built in what might be called the old Solari coastal style: harbour and drinking houses hugging the shoreline, the noble keep and city watch barracks set further west where the land curls south, and a great open square — used for markets, spectacles, and public gatherings alike — set deeper into the city proper. Despite its size, dwarfing every settlement the party had passed since leaving [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Amphos|Amphos]], Pithertara remains, by its own residents' account, a provincial city that bows to the capital in scale and importance.

## History

An ancient, blackened obelisk stands in the centre of Pithertara's market square, inscribed with dull red writing in a script no living resident can read. Local tradition holds that it dates to the era of [[Anparashi Puli Bralofai Blafadasi Komitani Ailushi|Puli Komitani]], roughly seven hundred years past, and is tied to the wider [[1082 IOG - Division of The Empire of Solara|Division of the Empire of Solara]], during which the imperial administration was relocated to Amphos. Why a monument to that refounding stands in Pithertara rather than the capital itself is not explained by anyone the party has asked; to most residents, it has simply always been there — a piece of civic furniture rather than a mystery.

The docks have also, more recently, been the site of harder history. It was on the same stretch of stone quay used by the Zoraneset that [[Dashos]] was seen dragged off in irons following the murder of Captain [[Mari Shkari]], and it is also where [[Masha]], held responsible for a fire she set at [[The Great Palace of Amphos]], was put to death by the city watch.

## Governance

Like other Solari cities, Pithertara is administered from its keep, a walled complex west of the harbour containing a smith, butcher, storage sheds, an armoury, and noble stables clustered around a central administrative hall — an ornate, gold-and-silver-dressed building of Doric proportion, evidently built some centuries ago in a more prosperous age. It was here that [[John Ironchest - Player Notes|John Ironchest]] secured travel papers for [[Ulriki]], paying a reduced veteran's rate of twenty gold on the strength of a service badge from the Solari Northern Border, after being quoted a standard fee of fifty. The keep's administrator was notably well informed on matters of state, including the ongoing diplomatic incident surrounding the disappearance of [[Ellowen Aurelian Vale|Lady Ellowen Aurelian Vale]].

## Districts and notable locations

### The docks

Pithertara's harbour district hugs the shoreline and hosts the bulk of the city's maritime trade, including the quay at which the Zoraneset came in to dock. It was here that the party first came ashore, and later loaded fresh provisions — including barrels of bread, grain, dried meat, and [[The Black Hare|John Ironchest's warhorse]] — before departing for Myrneleu.

### The market square

Deeper into the city lies Pithertara's great open square, host to markets, public spectacle, and — during the party's stay — a touring exhibition of the [[World Bodybuilding Order]], drawing crowds from three provinces to watch competitions in atlas stone lifting, wrestling, squats, keg tossing, and deadlifting. The square is also home to butcher's stalls and general market trade, and to the city's ancient obelisk.

### The Iron Chest

The inn at which the party lodged during their stay was, by the time they left, freshly repainted with a new sign depicting John Ironchest hoisting an atlas stone overhead, and renamed the Iron Chest in his honour. It was here that the party secured a shared room after some brief haggling over price, that a company of Solari watchmen shared news of the empire, of [[Duchy of Brackenfall|Brackenfall]], and of the capital's [[Manuscript on Tabaxi|small tabaxi population]], and that [[Riley - Player Notes|Riley]], through less than ideal circumstances, invented the [[Rileywich#Grilled Rileywich|Grilled Cheese Rileywich]].

### The farmland north of the city

North of Pithertara lies open farmland, harvested and quiet at the time of the party's visit. It was here that John Ironchest purchased grain and apples for the voyage ahead, in exchange for coin and a spot of unplanned home repair.

### The stables

Pithertara's stables housed the Black Hare and [[Kitty|the ship's cat]] during the party's stay, and were briefly investigated by [[Rhoda of the Shimmering Moontide - Player Notes|Rhoda]] and [[Evangeline Marquis|Yvaquis]], who found nothing amiss beyond a horse in evidently good condition.

## Notable events

Pithertara was the site of several significant developments in the party's journey:

- The recruitment of John Ironchest to the crew of the Zoraneset, following his dominant performance at the World Bodybuilding Order exhibition.
- The recruitment of Rhoda, encountered first as an itinerant drummer at the same exhibition.
- The discovery and partial reading of a burnt manuscript concerning tabaxi history, recovered by Ulriki from a crate John Ironchest salvaged en route to the city.
- The party's first encounter with [[Woubble]], found in the streets near the World Bodybuilding Order's tent.
- The formal reassignment of the Zoraneset's crew roles, undertaken at the docks immediately prior to departure.
