**Peles** (also known as **Pez** or **Pez Dispener**) is a young rower aboard [[The Zoraneset]] and a devoted follower of the [[World Bodybuilding Order|WBO]]. He is approximately seventeen years old and is employed as one of the ship's rowers under the captaincy of [[Big Beefy Bob - Player Notes|Big Beefy Bob]].

## Appearance
Peles is a young man of around seventeen with dusty blond hair. He has the makings of an average build, but malnutrition has left him meagre and underfed—his hands are described as skeletal and calloused, and his tunic is thread-bare. He wears fraying linen trousers and carries a brown capsa (a cylindrical scroll-case tied with a leather strap, used to protect important writings and illustrations), which is itself damaged by the salt spray all seafarers endure.

## Personality
Peles is nervous and fidgety, particularly in the presence of those he admires. When first confronted by [[John Ironchest - Player Notes|John Ironchest]], he trembled, shifted his weight, and had to catch himself from falling. His gaze repeatedly fell to his hands, and he fiddled with a small silver trinket to occupy his time.

Despite his physical frailty and evident poverty, Peles possesses a resilient and optimistic spirit. When John misnames him "Pez" on a signed figure, Peles interprets it not as an insult but as a gift—"A new name for a new me! A more confident me!" This ability to find meaning in small things suggests a character who has learned to make the most of what he is given.

His admiration for the WBO is sincere and long-standing. He follows the Order "however I can in my spare time wherever we make landfall," indicating a life of transient work and limited pleasures. His devotion is such that he carries a tarnished silver trinket—no larger than a thimble—embossed with the WBO's letters, and owns a licensed carved wooden figure of John Ironchest.

## History
Little is known of Peles's early life. He is employed as a rower aboard the Zoraneset, a position he describes simply as "the ship where I work." His previous employer did not pay him for his labour—a situation [[John Ironchest - Player Notes|John Ironchest]] notes is "all too common in the Eastern Solari Empire."

At some point, Peles acquired a licensed wooden figure of John Ironchest, complete with a WBO seal carved into its base. He also carries a smaller silver WBO trinket, tarnished with age and handling. These possessions suggest a long-standing dedication to the Order and a willingness to spend what little money he has on its merchandise.

## Relationships

### John Ironchest
Peles first encountered John Ironchest aboard the Zoraneset, where he began following the seven-foot strongman around the orlop deck, mimicking his actions and admiring his physique from a distance. John noticed immediately and eventually confronted him, leaping to the main deck and dropping back down behind the young rower.

When questioned, Peles confessed his admiration: "I just... love your work." He explained that he had been following the WBO for a long time and follows it whenever the ship makes landfall. His nervousness gave way to excited enthusiasm, declaring John "the best! The best on the ship! The ship where I work!"

John, though flattered, was honest about his transient presence: "I'm only helping out on this ship. I'm not here for the long haul. I have my own goals that I am pursuing." He promised, however, to remember Peles after his departure—a small kindness that gave the young rower significance.

The encounter culminated in John signing Peles's carved figure. When Peles gave his name, John—whether mishearing or mis-scribing—inscribed it "To Pez." Rather than being disappointed, Peles embraced the error as a new identity, declaring it "a new name for a new more confident me."

## Possessions
- WBO Trinket: A small silver trinket, tarnished and no larger than a thimble, embossed with the letters WBO. Peles carries it in his pocket and fiddles with it when nervous.
- Carved Figure of John Ironchest: A licensed wooden figure depicting John Ironchest, with a WBO seal carved into the base. It was signed by John himself, albeit with the name "Pez" rather than "Peles."
- Capsa: A brown cylindrical scroll-case tied with a leather strap, used to protect important writings and illustrations. It is damaged by salt spray. Its contents have not been revealed.
