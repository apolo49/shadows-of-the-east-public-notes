**Zenshoran Hagashar**, styled the *Donpotes da Tagaminase* ("Head of the Military") and known popularly as the **Lion of the East**, is a tabaxi general serving the [[Empire of Solara]] as commander of its imperial armed forces and of the [[Tagaminase]]. Born in [[Divine Empire of Daxing|Daxing]], he emigrated west roughly twenty-five years before the events of [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/index|Campaign 2]], rising quickly through the ranks to earn the personal trust of the Emperor. He is one of only two individuals of Tabaxi or part-Tabaxi descent recorded in the Bureau's 1770 IOG census of [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Amphos|Amphos]].

As Donpotes, Zenshoran commands the Tagaminase as a force distinct from the ordinary City Watch or the Vanguard, marked by its own crest and has brought knowledge from the east to field experimental weaponry. His private life is little documented: he has an unnamed daughter said to be travelling with [[The Prince - Player Notes|the Prince]]'s column, from whom he is reportedly estranged, and he is believed, though never confirmed, to have quietly intervened on behalf of the vigilante known in the slums as the [[Riley - Player Notes|Freedom Cat]], a connection he has never acknowledged.
# Appearance

He wears ornate [[Campaign 1 - Before the Start/DM Notes/Cultures/Solari|Solari]] lamellar armour over deep crimson silks from the Eastern Realms. He moves with a silent, predatory grace that contrasts sharply with his rigid, bureaucratic posture. His most distinguishing feature is a long, jagged scar running through his left ear. Commonly he is seen wearing a lion mask to mask his face and intimidate his enemies.
