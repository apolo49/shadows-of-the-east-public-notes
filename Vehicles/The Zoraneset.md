_The Zoraneset_ is a Solari dromon-class oared warship, and the current vessel of the party following [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Session Summaries/Session 5 - The Zoraneset|their departure from Aklebe]]. She is presently under the command of [[Big Beefy Bob - Player Notes|Big Beefy Bob]], having previously been captained by [[Mari Shkari]] until the latter's murder while at sea. The ship departed [[Pithertara]] on the [[Setting/Semantics/Dating/index|5th of Hanomi, 1782 IOG]], bound for [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Myrneleu|Myrneleu]] en route to [[Itria Province]].

## Overview

The Zoraneset is a large, oar-driven Solari warship of the dromon type, identifiable at a distance by the rows of shields mounted along her sides — the detail [[John Ironchest - Player Notes|John Ironchest]] used to pick her out from the rest of the Pithertara harbour when he joined the crew. She is equipped for both war and cargo, carrying a bow-mounted siphon and ballistae alongside holds large enough to transport livestock, warhorses, and bulk provisions.

A large rowing crew — some 150 bedrolls have been counted below decks, out of a total ship's complement of roughly 250 — powers her through harbours and coastal waters, while sail is raised once she reaches open sea. She cannot leave port under her own power; tug boats are required to draw her clear of the shore before the rowers and sail can take over. Below decks, oar-holes let in what little light reaches the rowing benches, and a single heavy drum — usually [[Rhoda of the Shimmering Moontide - Player Notes|Rhoda's]] — keeps the rowers in disciplined, unified rhythm.

A hand-painted image of the ship exists, originally kept by the harbourmaster in [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Aklebe|Aklebe]] and taken by [[Big Beefy Bob - Player Notes|Bob]] before the party's departure from that city. It has since proven useful for identifying the Zoraneset to new arrivals unfamiliar with her silhouette.

## Layout

- **Top deck** — the primary working deck, host to the ship's wheel and navigation instruments, the bow-mounted siphon and ballistae emplacement, and open space used for training, socialising, and (on at least one occasion) bodybuilding practice.
- **The kitchen** — located below the top deck; prone to sliding implements and general disarray whenever the ship gets underway, much to the alarm of its current cook.
- **The hold** — amidships, secured storage for livestock and heavy cargo. Presently houses [[The Black Hare]], the ship's cat, and [[Pupolas Crate]], alongside barrels of grain, bread, dried meat, and other provisions.
- **The rowing deck** — below decks, roughly 150 bedrolls' worth of berthing for the rowing crew, lit only by the oar-holes.

## History

### Under Mari Shkari

The Zoraneset previously sailed under Captain Mari Shkari. During the leg of the voyage prior to arriving at [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Pithertara|Pithertara]], the ship's stores were raided by [[Pupolas Crate]] and [[Thumpwick]], drawing the captain's considerable ire.

Shkari was murdered aboard the ship in the early hours of the 4th of Hanomi, 1782 IOG. [[John Ironchest - Player Notes|John Ironchest]], who was not yet aboard, had independently received a vision at an obelisk in a distant forest describing the ship's watch rotation that night in unsettling detail — two on watch at one o'clock, two at two o'clock, two at three o'clock, three at four o'clock, only one at five o'clock, and finally, at six o'clock, a scream. The pattern matched the Zoraneset's actual watch rota exactly, ending in Shkari's death. [[Dashos]] was subsequently held responsible and was seen dragged off in irons at the Pithertara docks.

### Change of command

Following Shkari's death, Big Beefy Bob — the ship's former first mate — was elevated to captain. The promotion was formalised at the Pithertara docks on the morning of the 5th of Hanomi, shortly before departure, with Ellowen overseeing the reassignment of the remaining senior roles:

- **First Mate:** Rhoda, for her natural charisma and command presence
- **Quartermaster:** Ellowen, for her navigational skill and background in cartography and international politics
- **Bosun:** John Ironchest, for his strength and self-taught carpentry
- **Surgeon:** [[Evangeline Marquis|Yvaquis]], continuing her prior role, drawing on her clerical training and botanical knowledge
- **Cook:** [[Riley - Player Notes|Riley]], inventor of the [[Rileywich]] and its variants

### Departure from Pithertara

The Zoraneset departed Pithertara on the morning of the 5th of Hanomi, 1782 IOG, under tow before making open water under oar and sail, bound first for Myrneleu and ultimately for Itria Province. Notable cargo taken aboard prior to departure included grain, bread, dried meat, and apples purchased by John Ironchest from a farmer north of the city, as well as The Black Hare, John's warhorse, whom the boarding plank proved more than capable of bearing.

During the crossing, the ship briefly strayed off course near the [[Shimmering Moonrise Reefs]] — home to Rhoda — after she recognised a distant glow beneath the waves as her home and went overboard to investigate, nearly losing the ship's rope back aboard in the process. A further navigational error, caused by a distracted quartermaster and a well-timed bowl of gruel, added a day's delay to the voyage before being corrected down to roughly four hours.

## Notable incidents

- **The murder of Mari Shkari**, foreseen independently by John Ironchest via visions from an obelisk before he had ever set foot aboard the ship.
- **The stores raid**, perpetrated by Pupolas Crate and Thumpwick prior to arrival at Pithertara.
- **Recovery of a sealed crate**, found by John Ironchest en route to Pithertara, later found to contain [[Manuscript on Tabaxi|a burnt manuscript concerning tabaxi history]], along with rum, herbs, and a supply of googly eyes — most of which he sold off from a stall on the Pithertara docks before departure.
- **[[Woubble]]'s seasickness**, treated by Ellowen with a Sleep spell during the crossing to Myrneleu.
- **The near-loss of Rhoda**, retrieved from the sea after nearly being separated from the ship's trailing rope near the Shimmering Moonrise Reefs.

## Gallery

A single known painting of the Zoraneset exists, recovered by Big Beefy Bob from a harbourmaster in Aklebe. It remains among his effects and has been used to help newcomers identify the ship in port.
